Study Session

CESSA: how the state’s mental-health dispatch law lands on Oak Park’s 911 system.

West Suburban Consolidated Dispatch Center (WSCDC) walks the Board through Community Emergency Services and Supports Act implementation — dispatch requirements, decision protocols, and the operational impact on the Village’s emergency response. CESSA reshapes how calls involving mental-health crises get routed; tonight’s presentation is about what that means at the dispatch board level.

ID 26-230

¶
Study Session

A pavement management plan, and the budget choices it forces.

CMAP’s consultant presents the Pavement Management Program developed under a 2025 technical assistance grant — a roadway condition assessment with budget scenarios for maintaining or improving the network. The recommendations feed directly into the upcoming budget and the 5-year Capital Improvement Plan. Tonight is the Board’s first look at the scenarios.
