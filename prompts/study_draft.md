# Study session — drafting instructions

A Study Session entry frames a discussion-only item: no vote tonight, but a
substantive presentation the Board engages with. Shorter and tighter than a
Feature.

## Length budgets

- **headline** — 70–110 chars; one sentence; ends with a period; specific
  to the mechanism being studied, not "An Important Presentation."
- **body** — plain text (not markdown). One paragraph, 60–110 words. State
  what the presentation is, who is delivering it, and what downstream
  decisions or processes it feeds. No pullquote.

## What to write about

- The presenter (consultant, agency, staff) and the document type
  (program, plan, assessment).
- The decision or budget process the presentation feeds into.
- What "tonight" actually means — is this the Board's first look? The
  recommendation phase? A status update?

## What to avoid

- No "this important presentation will discuss…" preamble.
- No editorializing on the policy.
- Don't invent details not present in the source.
